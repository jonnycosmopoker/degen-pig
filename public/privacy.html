Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy explains what Degen Pig collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • If you play without an account: your settings, names you type and Pass & Play stats are saved only on your own device.
  • If you sign in: your email address and the name on your sign-in (from email or Google), plus your game records: achievements, wins, rivals, table history and leaderboard results.
  • When you play online: the name you choose, your moves, and any table chat, so other players at your table can see them.
  • When you buy from the Shop: what you bought, when, and the amount. Your payment is handled by Stripe, which collects your card details and billing information under its own privacy policy. We never receive your full card number.
  • Basic technical data: like any website, our hosting provider handles your IP address and browser information to deliver the game and keep it secure.

How we use it

  • To run the game, save your progress and purchases, and show leaderboards.
  • To send sign-in links and purchase receipts.
  • To answer your support requests and keep the game fair and secure.

We don't sell your personal information, we don't show ads, and we don't share your information with advertisers.

Who helps us

We use a few service providers to run Degen Pig: Cloudflare (hosting), Supabase (sign-in), Resend (sign-in emails), Google Workspace (support email) and Stripe (payments). They only use your information to provide their service to us.

Your choices

  • You can play without signing in.
  • You can delete your account and game records at any time from the account menu in the game, or by emailing us.
  • You can ask us what information we have about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing support@degenpig.com.

Children

Degen Pig is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
