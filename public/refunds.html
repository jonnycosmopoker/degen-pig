Refund Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Everything in the Degen Pig Shop is a digital item that's added to your account right after you pay. We want you to be happy with what you buy.

Refunds within 14 days

If you bought something by mistake or you're not happy with it, email support@degenpig.com within 14 days of the purchase. Include the email on your account and what you bought. We'll refund it to your original payment method.

If something doesn't work

If an item you paid for doesn't show up in your Locker or doesn't work, email us. We'll fix it or refund it, even after 14 days.

What happens to a refunded item

When a purchase is refunded, the items from that purchase are removed from your account. If you refund part of a bundle deal, we may refund the whole purchase instead.

How long it takes

Once we approve a refund, your bank usually shows it within 5–10 business days.

Card disputes

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank; we can usually sort it out faster. If a dispute is decided in the cardholder’s favor, the items from that charge are removed.
